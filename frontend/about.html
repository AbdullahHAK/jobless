About Jobless

Why this exists, how the data actually gets here, and why it stays trustworthy.

The problem

Finding a tech job in Pakistan usually means checking dozens of individual company career pages by hand, because no single site reliably covers the local market the way LinkedIn or Indeed cover the US. Jobless exists to be that one place - real openings from every company it tracks, in one searchable list, updated every day without anyone manually re-typing a single listing.

How the data gets here

Every company on Jobless is a self-contained scraper that reads directly from that company's own career page or the public job-board API it already uses (Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, and similar widely-used hiring platforms). Nothing is typed in by hand and nothing is copied from a third-party aggregator - if a listing is on Jobless, it's because the company itself published it, and you can always trust the "Apply" button to take you to the real source.

A scheduled job runs every company's scraper once a day, stores the results, and republishes the dataset this site reads from. If one company's page changes format or goes down temporarily, that one scraper fails in isolation - it doesn't take the rest of the site's data down with it, and nothing gets deleted on a bad day. A listing only disappears from Jobless once that company's own scraper runs successfully again and confirms the posting is genuinely gone.

What "free, no login" actually means

There's no account system for browsing jobs, no paywall, and no resume upload required to see a listing or click apply. "Mark as applied" and the job filters all run entirely in your own browser - nothing about which jobs you've viewed or applied to is sent anywhere. The one optional exception is the email digest, which needs an email address to send to; everything else works with zero signup.

What Jobless won't do

No fabricated listings, no fake "X people applied" counters, and no scraping of review platforms like Glassdoor - company reviews on this site are written directly by the people who submit them, moderated before publishing, never pulled from somewhere else and relabeled as original. If a company's listings are genuinely thin or a review category has little real data, this site says so rather than padding it out.

Where this is headed

The goal is to become the single go-to destination for Pakistani tech jobs - starting from the companies already tracked today and growing toward 100 for a proper first launch. The whole project is open source; if a company is missing, anyone can suggest it or contribute a scraper directly.
